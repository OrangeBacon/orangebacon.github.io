a lot of the time i just want to be outside, away from all the noise and busyness of the world

pretending that there isn't anything else happening that i need to worry about,

that chance to disconnect from the world, from everything that i need to deal with

not having to think about work, deadlines, people trying to get in contact with me

just content, surrounded by nature, perhaps lying in a field or sitting in a forest

finally getting that peace and quiet that you just can't get while living in a city
