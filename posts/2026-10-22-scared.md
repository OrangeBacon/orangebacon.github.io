scared of change

yet scared of stagnation

scared of who you are

of what you can do

the decisions you can make

and the depths of those emotions

<br>

scared of the conflict in your mind

of the thoughts you have

that need to go back

to how things once were

finding comfort in the pain

waiting for it to all crash down

<br>

scared of how to accept help

when you can't tell what's wrong

making everything worse

the thoughts you have

scared of your self.
